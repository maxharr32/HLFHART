About HLFHART

Artist photo & signature style — add an image once it's ready.

Hi — I'm HLFHART. I make art that starts from a feeling, a mark, or a material I'm curious about. Some pieces begin as quick pencil-and-ink sketches; others grow slowly through layers of colour and texture.

This portfolio is a living collection. Below you'll find the main bodies of work: biro portraits drawn up in dense ink, acrylic paintings built from soft washes and bold blocks, postcards — small pieces made for sharing, life drawing that tracks the body in motion, collage assembled from found fragments, and sculpture that steps out of the frame.

Every piece on this site has a short note explaining what it's about. Pick something and click in — you might discover the story behind the mark.

If you'd like a piece commissioned, printed on something, or simply want to say hello, get in touch through the contact page.
